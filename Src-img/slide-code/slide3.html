Executive Summary

QA Engineering Assessment

GoSMS Integration

01

Core Objective

Assure absolute financial and data integrity across the quotation generation and SMS crediting workflow. The primary goal is to prevent financial discrepancy and ensure atomic transaction success between GoSMS and QuickBooks.

02

Assessment Scope

Comprehensive validation covering 5 distinct layers: User Interface inputs, complex Business Logic calculations, external QuickBooks API integration, Email Service delivery reliability, and Database transactional consistency.

03

Strategic Approach

Utilizing formal methods including State Transition Modeling and Failure Mode Analysis. The strategy emphasizes risk-driven testing, focusing on idempotency, concurrency handling, and fail-safe mechanisms for third-party integrations.

04

Expected Outcomes

Delivery of a clear, robust logical flow design with defined atomicity requirements. A complete catalog of professional test cases covering happy paths and edge cases, accompanied by a prioritized Risk & Mitigation Register.
